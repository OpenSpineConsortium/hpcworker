## Plan

`job replace` never strands a job. It reads the node features each pending job asks (squeue's `%f`) and races the job with them, over the partitions sinfo shows can run it: a node offering every feature, a time limit not under the job's. A job waiting on BadConstraints is raced too, before the others, and moved where it can run. A job sent without `--placement` stays unmoved, held by a test. Every move's line says why.
- framework/lib/jobs.sh: the features in job_own_jobs, the partitions' features and limits from one sinfo, the race of a queued job held to them, BadConstraints among the reasons raced and first, the why on each move's line.
- framework/tests/test_jobs.py and interface/testbed/fakebin/sinfo: the tests, written first, and the stand-in's answer to a format that asks for features.
- framework/README.md, framework/VERSION, framework/bin/hpcworker, framework/tests/test_status.py, interface/contract: framework 36.
