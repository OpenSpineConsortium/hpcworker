# The Full Edition agreement

There is no paid edition of Beethoven yet and no Full Edition text in this repository. When a paid
edition exists, its agreement will be Ashley's `extension/LICENSE-FULL.txt` (version 4, 2026-09-28)
adapted the same way `LICENSE.txt` here was adapted from Ashley's Trial Edition text: the same
structure, section numbers and dispute terms (sections 19 and 20 unchanged); the product description
changed to the extension plus the cluster-side framework; Wayne-State-specific access wording replaced
with "the institution's Open OnDemand portal and computing systems" while keeping the approved-list
model; the three Known Risks specific to this form factor (Anthropic changing or enforcing Remote
Control eligibility, the university disabling or reconfiguring Open OnDemand's shell, files or reverse
proxy, a browser store removing or disabling the extension) added to section 8; and the disclosure
sentences of section 5(e) carried over word for word. Its refund, term and outage-extension clauses
come from Ashley's Full Edition text unchanged. One thing differs by store: Apple's App Review
Guideline 3.1.1 forbids an app from using its own mechanism, such as a license key, to unlock
functionality, so the Safari edition is free or is sold by in-app purchase inside the host app, and
the Full Edition agreement (titled "Beethoven Full Edition License Agreement", as section 1 of the
Trial Edition text names it) will say so where it describes keys; the May 2025 change to US external
links did not remove that obligation. Before any paid key is sold, the written answers from Wayne State
C&IT and from Anthropic named in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` are on file, and the named licensor on the key,
the listings and the store accounts is the LLC once it exists.
